Every contribution helps keep the Farm Nerds running strong, from possibly purchasing new devices and ideas to implement to a farmstead, homestead type setting. We don't say full on farm right now because realistically it isn't that yet. Right now it is mainly completely barren land with nothing on it but mesquite trees, a small home, a small unattached garage/workshop not able to be used as a garage. And a carport that for now is a warehouse for the items we have gathered over the past 2 years to implement in reconstruction.
We have torn wall panels, ceilings, insulation, flooring in spots, to remove old rotted pieces. Entire roof and insulation replaced currently, basically every wall and siding insulation replaced. Removed old front door, replaced with a significantly larger homemade wooden door made by me, ol PapaBear. 
During this redo, I have added significantly more lightning, some rooms on had 1 o 2 lights, now a minimum or 4 in ceiling. We got with our local power company, got a larger main breaker, double checked that the previous main wire running underground to the home break is capable of what was added. Swapped the old home breaker box, new wiring in many rooms where it made sense to put it. 
Ran ethernet cables throughout since we had ceilings down., also run cable to the outside of the home for external WiFi access points. Because a metal shell on a home doesn't really like to allow wifi in or out. So we planned ahead for that venture.

Whether you want to make a one-time contribution or support monthly, everything helps keep the farm nerd magic cooking.

One of our ultimate goals is to do a '40 Acres, One network' install covering that process from start to finish.
The other shenanigans we get in to is just taking a tech device, engineering it a way to fit a problem for us.

Like creating an inexpensive water house wifi timer.
Like creating a wifi connected deer feeder that has a camera on the feeder, it being solar, and make the feeder's timer adjustable from a browser or phone app.
We have some 30 year old set of metal gates at the driveway. If you want that closed, which we do, every person gets in the car, drives up, gets out, open gate, get in car, get out of car, close gate, get in car, drive to town. We are getting older, my wife and I. And I have had neck fusion and low back fusion surgery, anyway, long story long, it is a chore just to go to the local mini store to get the grandkids a Gatorade or something. So I want to completely automate this process, even up to geofencing, so that when we are driving towards the driveway, the system picks up on of our phones and begins opening the gate ahead of time so that when you pull up you drive right in.
