FarmNerds is where tech meets the dirt. It is run by JD out of Stephenville, Texas, and covers three things: tech, homelabbing and homesteading. Where they overlap, that is FarmLabs.

Reviews here cover tools, equipment, software, farm equipment, techniques and builds. Each one gets a score out of 10 and a plain list of pros and cons.

The scripts and recipes behind the homelab posts live on [GitHub](https://github.com/farmnerds-net).
