FarmNerds is where tech meets the dirt. It is run by JD out of Stephenville, Texas, and covers three things: tech, homelabbing and homesteading. Where they overlap, that is FarmLabs.

Reviews here cover tools, equipment, software, farm equipment, techniques and builds. Each one gets a score out of 10 and a plain list of pros and cons. Sometimes we even create our own open-source scripts to help with problem we have where it doesn't seem as many others would take the time to do.
